# 02 — Brand Kit B (proposal, v1) — "Fulcrum-derived"

**Status:** PROPOSED by Claude (2026-07-17) for the **Design B** A/B variant. Benjamin approves/edits before build. Token names stay stable so a brand change is a one-file edit.

## 1. Brand idea (the B personality)

Where Design A is *precision at daylight* (calm, technical-drawing), Design B is **momentum and nerve**: bold, high-contrast, confident, a little loud. It says a modern shop moves fast and Tolera is the tool that keeps up. Same substance (speed + your own numbers + control), delivered with more voltage.

Register stays plain technical German, Sie-Form, verbs over adjectives — but headlines here are punchier and shorter, and drop the trailing period (Fulcrum style).

## 2. Wordmark & logo (direction, needs final art)

- Wordmark: **"tolera"** lowercase, Hanken Grotesk ExtraBold (matches Fulcrum's heavy lowercase logo weight), `ink`.
- Mark concept (same options as Design A so the two A/B builds can share a logo): **tolerance-band** (± band around a nominal line) — recommended — or a chamfered **t**.
- On black sections the wordmark inverts to white.
- **DPMA/EUIPO screening before launch** (positioning §8.2) applies to Tolera + Lens/Kontur/Kalk/Vergabe.

## 3. Color

Exactly the token palette in 01-DESIGN-SYSTEM §2. Hierarchy for B:

- **Black + white is the frame; color is the voice.** Unlike A (color used sparingly), B uses the four accents boldly — big colored section heads (green→orange→blue rotation), `lime` as the highlighter/CTA punctuation, full-black interstitials.
- `ai-purple` @55% still reserved **exclusively** for AI suggestions inside product screenshots — the „purple = KI schlägt vor" grammar is shared across both designs and the product itself. Marketing chrome in B never uses purple (its energy comes from green/orange/blue/lime instead).

## 4. Type

Hanken Grotesk (everything) + JetBrains Mono (retro "old software" + technical labels). Both OFL, self-hosted — no licensing dependency, no CDN (GDPR). Substitute for Fulcrum's licensed Gilroy.

## 5. Voice snapshots (from locked positioning copy — unchanged from A)

Angebot raus." · Capacity „Jede Anfrage ein Angebot." · AI „KI im Vier-Augen-Prinzip: Tolera schlägt vor — Sie entscheiden." · Trust „🇩🇪 Daten in Deutschland · DSGVO · keine Kreditkarte". Full copy + EN mirrors: `docs/analysis/POSITIONING-AND-MESSAGING.md` (repo root) — the copy source of truth for **both** designs; do not restate or rewrite.

For B, section heads are shortened to 2–4 colored words with a lime mark on the key word, e.g. **„Jede Anfrage** ein Angebot" (lime on „Jede Anfrage"). Long-form copy stays identical to A.

## 6. Imagery

Product UI on tilted device frames is the hero imagery (§5.2). DACH-native data always (Werkstoffnummer `1.4301`, mm, ISO 2768-m, `1.234,56 €`, German UI). Real shop photography only when it's our pilot shop, with permission.

## 7. Don'ts

Fulcrum's Gilroy font, assets, illustrations, retro-artifact images, or copy · real screenshots of legacy competitor software for the "old software" motif (draw our own crude mocks) · decorative purple · Denglisch · fake testimonials/logos/metrics · certification-look badges we don't hold.
