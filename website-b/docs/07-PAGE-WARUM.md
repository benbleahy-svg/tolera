# 07 — Warum spec (`/warum`) — Design B — the Old-vs-New page

Fulcrum's "Why" page is its best set-piece: a black hero, then an **Old-Software-vs-Fulcrum split** where each row contrasts an intentionally ugly legacy artifact against a bright Fulcrum card. This is an *excellent* fit for Tolera's pain-first positioning (Excel + tribal knowledge + lost RFQs vs Tolera). This page also absorbs the manifesto ideas (Design B has no separate Manifest page).

Reference: `why-1440-01-hero-black-constellation.jpg`, `why-1440-02-oldvsnew-real-human.jpg`, `why-1440-03-oldvsnew-secure-retro.jpg`, `why-1440-04-oldvsnew-adapting.jpg`, `why-1440-05-see-and-learn-black.jpg`. Copy from positioning §3/§9 raw material — **do not invent facts**; final copy written with Benjamin.

## Sections

1. **Black hero + constellation** (§5.6): `hero-xl` white head „Der schnellste Anbieter gewinnt" (or „Anders — von Grund auf"), sub „Die nächste Generation der Angebotserstellung." Animated particle field. Reduced-motion → static.
2. **The split band** — a full-width `lime` band label row: left „Bisher" (mono, gray) · right „**Tolera**" (bold). (Fulcrum's „Old Software / fulcrum".)
3. **Old-vs-New rows** (§5.5) — each row: **left** a crude, our-own retro artifact of the status quo; **right** a bright `green-wash` card with a lime-marked colored header + body + „Mehr erfahren →". Proposed rows (map to positioning pains):
   - **Angebote in Excel** — left: a fake spreadsheet/Win-95 „ANGEBOT_final_v3.xls" with error dialog. right (green): „**Kalkuliert** in Minuten" — Ihre Sätze, Ihre Maschinen, ein Klick zum Angebot.
   - **Wissen im Kopf** (key-person risk) — left: a „Mitarbeiter nicht verfügbar" retro dialog. right (blue): „**Wissen im System**" — Kalkulationslogik gehört dem Betrieb, nicht einem Kopf.
   - **RFQs stapeln sich** — left: an overflowing inbox / „247 ungelesen". right (orange): „**Jede Anfrage** beantwortet" — Sie triagieren nicht mehr, welche Anfrage die Mühe wert ist.
   - **Server & IT** — left: a „Risky: buy a server, patch everything" dialog. right (green): „**In Deutschland gehostet**" — DSGVO, AVV, keine eigene IT.
   - **Kontrolle vs. Blackbox** — left: a „KI hat entschieden" scare box. right (blue): „**Vier-Augen-Prinzip**" — Tolera schlägt vor, Sie entscheiden. (purple accent inside the mini-mockup only.)
   - Each right-card header uses the section-color rotation + lime mark.
4. **Adapting / vision** (`why-1440-04` right card): „**Der schnellste gewinnt**" — every technology shift rewards the shops that adopt early. (Manifesto tone folded in — from positioning §9 background, kept honest.)
5. **See & learn more — black** (`why-1440-05`): white lime-marked head „Sehen & verstehen" + links to the Walkthrough and (later) demo video. Podcast/press icons omitted unless real.
6. **Schedule/CTA** — inline scheduler or lime CTA „Demo vereinbaren" + footer.

## Rules
- Retro "before" artifacts are **our own crude mocks** — never screenshot real competitor/legacy software.
- No competitor is named (positioning Decision #1: no explicit comparisons). The "old" side is the *status quo* (Excel, paper, tribal knowledge), not a named product.
- No invented metrics; manifesto claims stay values-level, not numeric.

## Acceptance
- Old-vs-new split reads instantly at 1440/768/390 (stacks to before-then-after on mobile, `mobile-390-03` pattern); lime marks animate in (static under reduced-motion).
- Every "after" claim traces to positioning copy; no named competitors; retro art is original.
