# 06 — KI page spec (`/ki`) — Design B — the "Archie"-equivalent

Fulcrum's Archie page sells its AI (Ask / Build / Agents). Tolera's AI is **Lens** + the **AI-Governor** ("KI im Vier-Augen-Prinzip"). This page is Tolera's AI story in Fulcrum's high-energy AI-page layout — **but honest to what Tolera actually does.**

**Critical honesty note:** Fulcrum's Archie pitches "ask anything," "build custom apps," and "agents (coming soon)." Tolera does **not** have a general chat-your-ERP, an app-builder, or autonomous agents. Do **not** copy that story. Tolera's AI = *reads RFQs/drawings and suggests structured data + DFM flags, always under human accept.* Keep every claim to that. Anything beyond → `OPEN:` in DECISIONS.md, not on the page.

Reference: `archie-1440-01-hero-live-demo.jpg`, `archie-1440-02-built-secure-black.jpg`, `archie-1440-03-chat-split.jpg`, `archie-1440-05-cta-lime.jpg`. Copy from positioning §3 (AI control line) and §6 (AI posture).

## Sections

1. **Hero** — centered `hero` head (PROPOSED: „KI, die vorschlägt. Sie entscheiden.") + `lead` sub: „Tolera liest Ihre Anfragen, Zeichnungen und STEP-Dateien und macht daraus kalkulierbare Positionen — jede Übernahme bestätigen Sie selbst." Centered, big, on a soft tint.
2. **Live extraction demo** — Fulcrum's „LIVE INTERACTIVE DEMO" chip + framed app (`archie-1440-01`). For Tolera: a **framed Lens extraction demo** — a German RFQ email with purple 55% suggestions and „Übernehmen" buttons (asset P0-2). If a real interactive tour isn't ready, a captioned video/screenshot with the „LIVE" chip removed (don't imply live if it isn't).
3. **„Vier-Augen-Prinzip"** — `section` head (blue) with lime mark. Two-column: left claim „Tolera schlägt vor — Sie entscheiden.", right a mockup showing a suggestion being accepted. The AI-Governor purple grammar is the visual. This is the differentiator; give it the most space.
4. **What Lens reads** — colored-outline cards (§5.4): „E-Mail & PDF" · „Zeichnungen" · „STEP / 3D" · „Stücklisten" — each with a cropped example. Capability claims only.
5. **Built secure — black interstitial** (`archie-1440-02` pattern). Tolera version: „In Deutschland gehostet. Ihre Daten bleiben Ihre." — DSGVO, AVV, Daten in Deutschland. **Do NOT copy Fulcrum's „ITAR / CMMC" claims** (US defense compliance — not Tolera's posture). Use only compliance Tolera actually holds/plans; unverified → omit.
6. **Never hallucinated** — short reassurance block: „Kein erfundener Wert. Was nicht auf der Zeichnung steht, erfindet Tolera nicht." (mirrors build-spec Lens rule; positioning-consistent).
7. **CTA** — big lime pill „Lens im Rundgang ansehen →" (→ Walkthrough) + „Demo vereinbaren".

## Rules
- No „ask anything / build apps / agents" claims. No ITAR/CMMC. No autonomy language — everything is suggest-then-accept.
- Purple only inside the product screenshots (AI-Governor), never as page chrome.
- Naming: „Lens" is the feature brand; „KI" is the page label. Trademark screening applies (positioning §8.2).

## Acceptance
- Energy/parity with `archie-1440-*` frames (hero + live-demo frame + black secure section + lime CTA) at 3 breakpoints.
- Zero claims beyond Lens's real capabilities; a reviewer can trace each to the build spec or positioning doc.
