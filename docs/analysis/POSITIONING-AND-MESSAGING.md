Tier 4 (analysis/rationale) — the *binding* pieces (indicative public pricing, packaging, public feature brands) are logged in `DECISIONS.md` [2026-07-17]; this document carries the full reasoning, copy, and asset plan for the marketing material.

## 1. Positioning statement

> For owners of German SME metal job shops (Lohnfertiger, Blech + Zerspanung, ~5–50 people) who lose work because quoting takes days, **Tolera turns an inbound RFQ — drawing, STEP file, or email — into a priced quote in minutes, calculated from the shop's own machines, rates, and processes.** Unlike quoting today (Excel, tribal knowledge, unanswered RFQs), Tolera lets a shop answer every RFQ fast enough to win it.

## 3. Message hierarchy & core copy

### Hero (pain-led, variant C1)

- **DE headline:** „**Verlieren Sie keine Aufträge mehr an schnellere Anbieter.**"
- **EN headline:** "Stop losing work to faster shops."
- **DE sub-line:** „Tolera macht aus jeder Anfrage in Minuten ein Angebot — kalkuliert mit Ihren Stundensätzen, nicht mit Durchschnittswerten."
- **EN sub-line:** "Tolera turns every RFQ into a priced quote in minutes — calculated from your rates, not from averages."
- CTA: „14 Tage kostenlos testen" / trust strip: „🇩🇪 Daten in Deutschland · DSGVO · keine Kreditkarte"
- A/B candidate for later: „Verlieren Sie keine Aufträge mehr an Schnellere." (C2, punchier).

### Problem section (the lost-order story)

Opens: „**Der Auftrag ging an den, der zuerst geantwortet hat.**" Three beats: the RFQ arrives → the week happens (Excel, callbacks, the pile) → the competitor answers first. Kicker: „**Der schnellste Anbieter gewinnt — auch wenn er nicht der günstigste ist.**"

### Product punch (over the demo video)

„**Zeichnung rein. Angebot raus.**" / "Drawing in. Quote out." — deliberately *not* the hero; it headlines the video section where the product proves it, and doubles as the brandable slogan.

### Capacity section

„**Jede Anfrage ein Angebot.**" / "Every RFQ answered." — the deepest claim: you stop triaging which RFQs are worth the effort. Secondary theme lands here: quoting knowledge captured in the system, not in one person's head (key-person risk / succession — emotionally real for a GF).

### AI control line (wherever AI is described)

„**KI im Vier-Augen-Prinzip: Tolera schlägt vor — Sie entscheiden.**" / "AI on the four-eyes principle: Tolera suggests — you decide."

### Reserve copy (drawer, for ads/social)

„Anfragen stapeln sich. Aufträge gehen woanders hin." / "RFQs pile up. Orders go elsewhere." — quieter pain opener (variant B), good for display/social where an imperative reads too hard.

## 6. Feature-description philosophy (Attio-derived)

1. **The screenshot is the claim; the copy is the caption.** Never argue a feature works — show the populated UI and label it. Consequence: **every marketing screenshot must show DACH-native data** — Werkstoffnummer `1.4301`, mm, ISO 2768-m, German operations (Laserschneiden, Abkanten), `1.234,56 €` formatting, German UI strings. The screenshot *is* the positioning.
2. **Product as actor, owner as beneficiary.** Formula: *[what Tolera does, active verb] + [with YOUR data/rates] + [control clause where AI is involved]*. Verbs: liest, erkennt, kalkuliert, markiert, schlägt vor.
3. **Group by workflow stage, never by subsystem.** The taxonomy (also the nav dropdown, one-line tagline each):
   1. **Anfrage rein** — email ingest, extraction, BOM detection, triage (Lens)
   2. **Teil verstehen** — 3D viewer, feature recognition, DFM, no CAD seat (Kontur)
   3. **Kalkulieren** — rates, DIN/EN materials, processes, margin targets (Kalk)
   4. **Anbieten & gewinnen** — white-label quote PDF, digital quote, email threading, requote diff
   5. **Abwickeln** — vendor RFQ (Vergabe), DATEV, XRechnung/ZUGFeRD, GoBD
4. **Do NOT copy Attio's abstraction register.** No category coinage ("agentic revenue"-style) — concrete German nouns only. Keep the layout discipline, drop the hype vocabulary.

**Two deliberate deviations from Attio:**
- **Light theme, not dark.** Dark glassmorphism reads "SaaS for SaaS people"; this reader trusts daylight, paper, precision. Keep Attio's card/shadow/radius system (8–12px radius, soft shadows, cropped shots without browser chrome), invert the palette.
- **Purple is reserved exclusively for AI suggestions** — same rule as the product (AI-Governor purple, 55% opacity). The visual grammar „purple = KI schlägt vor" stays consistent from marketing through onboarding.

## 9. Competitive context (background, not for copy)

- **Uptool** (uptool.com, US, founded 2024, $6M seed — Khosla/Bessemer/Eclipse/Kleiner) converged on the same story for the US: "Speed Wins" → "Stop losing work to faster shops" → "2 minute quotes" → "Increase win rate" → "Never miss RFQs", testimonials with metrics, book-a-demo, no visible product. **Validation of the lane; US-only, so DACH stays open.** We beat their page by: showing the product (demo video), publishing prices, self-serve trial. For investors, Uptool is the pattern-match comp on the EN side.
- **Spanflug Make** (~€300/mo entry, CNC-only), **Tempus** (laser), **Stella Source** (sheet metal): per Decision #1 none are named or compared against in customer copy. Differentiation surfaces only as product truth („Ihre Stundensätze, nicht Durchschnittswerte").
- **Paperless Parts**: never mentioned publicly (also per white-label posture, `DECISIONS.md` [2026-06]).
- **247TailorSteel „Sophia"**: the DACH buyer's mental benchmark for instant quoting — our demo video plays against that expectation.
