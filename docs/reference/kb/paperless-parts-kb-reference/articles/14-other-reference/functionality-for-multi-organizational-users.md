# Functionality for multi-organizational users

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/functionality-for-multi-organizational-users  
> Topic: Other / Reference

If you're a user that needs to access information about your company across multiple organizations in Paperless Parts, there are two primary ways that you can jump from one organization to another:

- Switching organizations from the Account menu
- Viewing a cross-organizational notification

Note that this feature needs to be set up by a member of our team. If you're a multi-org user and are interested in this feature, reach out to your Customer Success Manager or [support@paperlessparts.com](mailto:support@paperlessparts.com) to get started.

# Using the Switch organization action:

1. Click on your name in the top right corner to open the **Account** menu.
2.   1. Tip: Use search to quickly locate the correct organization.

1. Select the organization you would like to switch to and click **Switch Organization** to confirm. Your browser will reload and you will be moved to the destination organization.

# Notifications across organizations

If you're a multi-org user, your notifications tab will include messages from all organizations that you're a team member of - regardless of which one you are *currently*logged into. Each notification will include a label letting you know where it is coming from, and selecting a notification will take you to that organization.

Jake, for instance, is a team member of both 3D CAD Consultant and A Cut Above CNC. Right now, he's viewing his notifications from A Cut Above CNC, which is why the organization label for the first notification is greyed in.
