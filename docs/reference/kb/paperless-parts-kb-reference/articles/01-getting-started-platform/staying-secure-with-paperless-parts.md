# Staying secure with Paperless Parts

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/staying-secure-with-paperless-parts  
> Topic: Getting Started & Platform

At Paperless Parts, protecting your data is one of our highest priorities. We use industry-leading security practices to safeguard our platform; security is also a shared responsibility. This article provides guidance on how you and your team can stay safe while using Paperless Parts or any online service.

## What You Can Expect From Us

- We will never ask you to change payment details by email.
  - Payment instructions are on your invoice.
  - If you ever receive an email that appears to change how you should pay Paperless Parts, treat it as suspicious and validate directly with our Finance team using the contact information on your invoice.
- We will never request that you send us passwords, login information, or proprietary files via email.
- We will never send you a password reset link unless you specifically request it through the Paperless Parts application.
- If you’re ever unsure whether a message claiming to be from Paperless Parts is legitimate, contact our support team through the app or by emailing [support@paperlessparts.com](mailto:support@paperlessparts.com).

## Best Practices for Account Security

1. Enable single Sign-On (SSO) if available
  - If your organization uses a single sign-on solution, we strongly recommend connecting your Paperless Parts account through it. SSO helps ensure strong authentication and centralized control.
2. Use strong passwords + Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  - For accounts not using SSO, create a strong, unique password that isn’t reused elsewhere.
  - Always turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for an additional layer of protection. See the [Two-factor authentication KB article](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/two-factor-authentication-2fa).
3. Be cautious with emails
  - Do not share sensitive information (passwords, proprietary data, or financial details) over email. Paperless Parts will never ask for this information outside of the product itself. Never include sensitive production data, customer data, or credentials in support tickets or emails. If you need to share examples, redact sensitive information first.
  - Watch for phishing attempts: emails that pretend to be from us or another trusted company but try to trick you into clicking links or providing information.
4. Validate unusual requests
  - If you receive an unexpected request related to your Paperless Parts account, especially regarding billing or security, confirm it with us directly.
  - You can always contact our team directly before taking action.
5. Maintain appropriate application permissions
  - Ensure only authorized users have access to your account. See [team members and permission KB article](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/team-page-and-user-permissions).
  - Deactivate users from Paperless Parts when they leave your organization.
  - Avoid provisioning users as Admins when a standard role would suffice.
6. Keep your devices and browsers secure
  - Make sure your computer, browser, and antivirus software are kept up-to-date with the latest patches.
  - Avoid accessing your account from shared or public computers whenever possible.

## What To Do If You Suspect an Issue

- If you think your account has been compromised, reset your password immediately, and notify your IT/security team so they can check for broader impact.
- Enable 2FA if it isn’t already active.
- If you have concerns, please contact our support team through the app or by emailing [support@paperlessparts.com](mailto:support@paperlessparts.com).

## Our Commitment

We are committed to keeping your data safe. By combining the safeguards we’ve built into our platform with the practices outlined above, you’ll help ensure that both your information and your customers’ information remain protected.
