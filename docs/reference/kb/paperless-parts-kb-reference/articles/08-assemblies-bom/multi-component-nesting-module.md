# Multi-component nesting module

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/multi-component-nesting-module  
> Topic: Assemblies & BOM

**Note:** A member of our team needs to configure these features before they can be turned on. To learn more, or to inquire about adding nesting to your account, [reach out to our Support team](mailto:mailto:support@paperlessparts.com).

### [Multi-component sheet metal nesting](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/multi-component-sheet-metal-nesting)

Optimize costs for multiple parts that can be nested together on a **sheet** of material.

### [Multi-component linear nesting](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/multi-component-linear-nesting)

Optimize costs for multiple parts that can be nested together on a **linear**material, such as bar, rod, tube, angle, or u-channel.
