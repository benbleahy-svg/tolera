# The digital quote

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/the-digital-quote  
> Topic: Quoting Workflow (Build a Quote)

All quotes that you finalize and send to a buyer through Paperless Parts will include a link to a digital version of your quote. ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6479377faef24e1deb40dafc/file-We3I4VL2tq.png)

Unlike sending your buyer a traditional PDF quote, sending a link to a digital quote gives you insight into whether or not your buyer has [interacted with the quote](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/tracking-outstanding-quotes), so you can know when to follow up on outstanding work. Plus, links (from a trusted and verified source) are more secure than email attachments.

The digital quote is also interactive, allowing buyers to make selections and place an order directly from the page (depending on your [checkout settings](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/accounting-settings) and the buyer's [payment terms](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/accounts-and-contacts#account)).

*Note*: If your buyer needs to review a specific price with their purchasing team before placing an order, they can make a selection and then select **Share**to copy the link. Navigating to that link will take you to the digital quote with the same selections.

## FAQ

### My customer viewed the digital quote but sent me a PO instead of using the digital checkout. How do I put the PO in my account?

You can f [acilitate orders from within your account](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/order-facilitation) if you receive a PO outside of Paperless Parts.

### **Is there a feature to attach material certifications to quotes or orders?**

For security reasons, we do not allow attachments on the emails that come out of Paperless Parts. However, if you host the material certifications on your website, you can include that URL in the body of the email or in the [customer-facing quote notes](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/quote-notes#customer).

### **Why is the logo on my digital quote blacked out?**

If your facility's logo appears to be blacked out on the Digital Quote (either on the PDF or the digital version), the file you used likely has a transparent background. To resolve the issue, try adding an opaque background or taking a screenshot of the logo and reuploading it to your [Facility Information](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/company-settings#facility)settings.
