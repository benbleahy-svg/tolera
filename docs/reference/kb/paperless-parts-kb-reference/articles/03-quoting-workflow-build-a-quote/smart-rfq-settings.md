# Smart RFQ settings

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/smart-rfq-settings  
> Topic: Quoting Workflow (Build a Quote)

Once you've [added the Smart RFQ form](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/the-smart-rfq-form) to your website, customize the fields available to your buyers and make sure the email confirmation is coming from the correct member of your team in the "Smart RFQ Settings" section of your settings.
![image.png](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001PUD&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000002zhA9)

# Smart RFQ confirmation email

When one of your buyers sends a submission through the smart RFQ form, they'll automatically receive an email thanking them and confirming their submission.

Smart RFQ auto-response emails will appear to be sent by a member of your team. You can assign the outgoing email address from your [Smart RFQ settings](https://app.paperlessparts.com/settings/quote_creation/smart_rfq_settings).

*Note**:* Only verified users can be marked as the "RFQ Confirmation Sender". If you do not see a user appear in this dropdown, confirm that they have [verified their email address](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/improving-email-deliverability) .

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001PUD&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000002zX4M)

*Note*: The phone number on the confirmation email will be the number associated with your facility. You can change your facility's phone number under [Company settings](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/company-settings#facility)**.**

# Smart RFQ fields

Select fields in this section to make them available to your buyers on the Smart RFQ form. Any field not presented on the form will need to be manually entered into the quote by a member of your team after a submission is made.

**Note -** When the 'Process' field is hidden on the RFQ form, you must select a Process to use for Material and Finish selection. This will not be visible to customers of the SmartRFQ.
