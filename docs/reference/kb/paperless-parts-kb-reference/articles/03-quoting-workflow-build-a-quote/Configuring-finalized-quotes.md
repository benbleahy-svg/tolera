# Configuring finalized quotes

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Configuring-finalized-quotes  
> Topic: Quoting Workflow (Build a Quote)

Finalized quote display settings enable your shop to control how quote information is displayed to buyers on both the Digital Quote and Quote PDF. This document provides a breakdown of what each setting does and how your selections may influence the final product you send to your buyer.

## Display Settings

Note that these settings apply to both the quote PDF and Digital Quote.

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns000000746v&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs00000HmOB9)

- Quote settings: Show/hide the Quote Number, RFQ Number (if applicable), Facility Phone Number, and Facility Website.
- Total and subtotal display: Choose whether to show a price range, single value (the maximum price), or nothing (will only display the unit price on each line item)
  - If "Price Range" or "Maximum Price" is selected, users can choose whether or not to display the total and subtotal/tax rate.
  -
- Select which team member(s) to display as the preparer and contact: Who to display as the “Prepared by” contact - the estimator or salesperson assigned to the quote, or both.
- Quote notes placement: Where to display Quote Notes (above or below line items).
- Line item settings: Show/hide the Part File Name, Part Thumbnail, 3D Part Preview, Part Dimensions, Process, Material, and Finish.
