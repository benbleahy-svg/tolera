# Quote Setup

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Quote-Setup  
> Topic: Quoting Workflow (Build a Quote)

This article will walk you through the steps to take in Paperless Parts to set up a quote to be ready for estimating. There are three main steps to setting up a quote: forwarding an email to start a quote, creating line items, and entering part information, which are discussed in detail below.

On this page:

- Quick start guide
- Email Forwarding
- Creating line items
  - Drag and drop from the Quote Files section
  - In bulk using a provided list of line items
  - Editing line items
- Entering Part Information and AI Suggestions
- FAQ

---

# **Quick start guide**

## *For more detailed walkthroughs and tutorial videos, see the***Walkthrough sections***below.*

1. Forward an email into Paperless.
  - Add a forwarding address in your Paperless account.
2. Open the new quote and organize your files in the Quote Files section.
  - You can  **rename, unzip, and edit the ITAR status** of files.
3. Create line items.
4. Fill out the **basic part information**in the viewer.

Bulk Create Line Items

## **Video Walkthrough**

## **Written Walkthrough**

You can [create line items](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/setting-up-quote-items) in bulk by selecting the "Bulk Create Line Items" button. If you have not yet created line items on the quote, we will make suggestions for line items based on the contents of your RFQ email.
![bulk create suggestions.png](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs0000038xpD)

Even if you had already created some line items or if there are no suggestions from the RFQ email, you can still click **Add Line Item** --> **Bulk create line items**.
![bulk create menu.png](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs0000039Cuf)

Copy/paste from your RFQ files, or type in part numbers, revisions, descriptions, and quantities in the spreadsheet-like interface. If we found any line item [suggestions](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Assistance) in the RFQ email, you will see this interface pre-populated with data. You can still edit any cell, delete cells, and add new rows.

![full suggestions bulk create.png](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs0000039D5x)
Clicking “create line items” creates manual line items (line items without files) in the order entered.

Based on filenames and AI suggestions, files in [Quote Files](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/New-actions-for-Quote-Supporting-Files-August-31st-2023) will be automatically analyzed to determine if they belong to one of the line items created. Click “Add to line items” to move the Quote Files into matching line items.

You can still [manually drag files](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/setting-up-quote-items) from Quote Files or your desktop into line items if you do not want to use the automation or to organize files that are not automatically added to line items:

You can move all of the files on a line item back to Quote Files by dragging the line item into the Quote Files section.
![remove from quote drag and drop.gif](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs0000039tGU)
Supporting files can be converted back to Quote Files or into a different line item if there are any mistakes made. Just select the line item and click on the 3 dots next to the supporting file, then select "Move to quote files."

## Editing Line Items

To [reorder line items](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/setting-up-quote-items) , drag a line item in between two other line items. Once you see the blue line between the two line items, you can drop the file, and it will reorder between those two line items.

![Reordering line items single.gif](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000001b1Xe)

To reorder multiple line items, drag the line items in between two other line items. Once you see the blue line, you can drop the files and they will reorder in between those two other line items.

![Reordering line items bulk.gif](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000001b1Fu)

To bulk update quantities, click on the checkboxes next to the line items you want to adjust quantities for. Then, click on the Actions button at the top of the left sidebar, and select "Update quantities." Next, select if you want to either "Add quantities" or "Replace all quantities," and then you can fill in the quantities. Just as a reminder, quantity specific overrides in your operations do not get extended to the new quantities.

![Bulk update QTYs.gif](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001ZDR&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000001b0TY)

# FAQ

- Does everyone on my team need their own forwarding address to forward RFQs into Paperless?
  - No. Everyone on your team can use the same forwarding address, but everyone on your team does have to create their own contact in their email client.
- Do I need to fill in the part #, revision, & quantities when creating line items?
  - No, Paperless Parts will default to populating line items with a quantity of at least 1. Also, the part number and revision fields are not required to create the line item.
- Can I move a line item back to the Quote Files section?
  - Yes, line item creation from Quote Files is a reversible action in the platform. Just drag the line(s) back to the Quote Files section and all of the files will will repopulate in the Quote Files section. Also, if you just want to move a supporting file, select the line item, navigate the files, click the three dots, and click "Move to quote files."
- What is a Pack and Go file?
  - A Pack and Go is a packaged set of files that define the geometry and structure of an assembly part. Also known as an assembly instruction file (.sldasm, .asm, .prt, .iam, .catproduct, .session).
- What is the Pack and Go icon next to the file name?
  - This means we’ve identified the file as a Pack and Go. If there is a caution sign in the Pack and Go icon, your assembly file is missing some information. If the Pack and Go is incomplete (the user only uploads an instruction file or does not upload all of the geometric files referenced by the instruction file), Paperless Parts will flag that you have uploaded an incomplete Pack and Go and that you will need to reupload with all of the necessary files.
- Is there a way to add RFQs without using email forwarding?
  - Yes. Create a new quote, download the RFQ in your email client, then drag the downloaded RFQ into the Quote Files section on the new quote.
- What is an AI suggestion?
  - Whenever you see the purple stars, Paperless Parts is suggesting a field via our [AI extractions](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Assistance). Our AI model scans prints to identify key words to provide suggestions for part number and revision.
- Will there always be AI suggestions?
  - No. Certain PDF part files are older or not as “clean” and the AI is not able to read the file.
- How do I add multiple lines to my description?
  - Press the "enter" button. If that does not work, try "shift + enter."
- Do AI Suggestions scan all files?
  - We only scan PDF files for part number, revision, description, material, and material specifications.
- Do supporting files get scanned?
  - Yes if it is a PDF part file.
- How do users get notified when they forward an RFQ into Paperless?
  - They will get an in-app notification and a notification via email. They can change who will get an email notification with the "Email Notification Settings" tab in settings.
- Where should I enter the process, material, and finishes for line items in this new workflow?
  - There are many ways to enter the process, material, and finishes in the new workflow. The recommended place is in the part viewer while viewing the prints, which can be done for root parts and child components (see “Entering Part Information and AI Suggestions”, above). You can also enter this information on the Build-a-Quote page for each line item. Lastly, you can enter this information in bulk for line items and child components by selecting more than one line item or child component and clicking "Actions" then update process/material/finish.
  - With all of these new ways to enter this information once the files are uploaded into Paperless, there is no longer a need to open your files before you upload them. Opening files in the secure Paperless Parts platform rather than downloading them to your computer adds another layer of protection against malware.
  - If you do wish to enter the process, material, and finish when uploading files as line items, you can enable these fields in the settings page under Quote Creation --> Line Item Setting, or going to [this link](https://app.paperlessparts.com/settings/quote_creation/line_item_settings).
