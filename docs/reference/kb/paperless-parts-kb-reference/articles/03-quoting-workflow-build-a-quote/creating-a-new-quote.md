# Creating a new quote

There are two ways to create a new quote in your account:

- Forward in an email RFQ.
- Manually create a new quote in Paperless Parts.

## Forward in an email RFQ

 If your buyer sent you an email RFQ with all of the part files attached, you can quickly get this information into Paperless Parts using email forwarding. Simply forward the email RFQ to your account's unique forwarding address (which you can copy from your Quote Creation settings).Once we receive and process the email, a new quote will appear in your Quotes list and Dashboard. Quotes created via email forwarding will show the subject line of the forwarded email alongside the Quote number. Any email attachments will be added to the Quote Files section of the quote, including a copy of the forwarded email itself, which will be marked as the quote's Original RFQ. We'll also send an email notification confirming that a new quote has been created in your account.

- Learn how to configure notifications for forwarded emails here.

## Manually create a new quote

1. Navigate to the Quotes page.
2. Click Create quote in the top right corner.

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/Mknb6PToUGCNLWGa1CCLm51dl1EswMf4eNYlE14uXJJIiqkQALmB2FeQ1e0fa4X1-BeYlNlqGOA636ZPNtw7meKYfTg9u8i0rt5nOqjm4B7Njg3Bd6Ci7OrgGvqfJuVp6IMtmYQWIBV4GUt0nNXig8k)

This will create a blank quote. As soon as you perform an action on the quote (assigning an RFQ number, adding a quote item, etc.) the quote will automatically be assigned the next available quote number and a new entry will be created in your **Quotes** tab.

# Quote-level information

Let's start in the **General** section of the Build-a-Quote page, where you can capture important information about the quote and the associated RFQ. There are 10 fields total, some of which are optional and some of which are required to finalize and send the quote.
If your quote was created from an email, some of these fields will autofilled or suggested based on the contents of the email. Keep an eye out for purple sparkles, which will indicate any field that has a suggestion available.

- Learn more about AI and suggestions here .

![image.png](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001EKH&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000002qgOb)

1. RFQ number: This is where you can input the RFQ number you may have received from your buyer. Optional
2. Send from facility: The facility you will be sending the parts from. Required
  1. This field is only visible if you have multiple facilities listed in Paperless Parts. Otherwise, it will default to the facility you entered in your Company Settings.
3. Quote due: The date by which you need to send the quote back to the customer. Required
  1. This date will automatically populate based on when you started the quote and the default time to quote, which can be set in the Shop Variables section of your settings. If a quote exceeds its Quote Due date it will be marked overdue and listed first in the Quotes tab.
4. Quote expiration: The date when the price on a quote is no longer valid. Required
  1. If an outstanding quote expires, online checkout will be disabled and replaced with the option to request a requote. Similar to the Quote Due date, this date will automatically populate based on the default quote expiration value, which can be set in the Shop Variables section of your settings.
5. Priority: A ranking system (1-10) of quote priority. Optional
  1. Use this field to differentiate between potentially hot work or RFQs that can sit in the queue for a bit. Regularly assigning quotes a priority will allow you to filter your dashboard by priority or set up saved filters to quickly view hot-button opportunities.
6. RFQ received: When an RFQ came into your shop. Use this field to keep track of how long your buyer has been waiting to get their quote. Optional
  1. If your quote was created from an email, this field will be suggested based on the date and time that your shop received the original email RFQ.
7. Contact: The person who will be receiving the quote. Type to search by first name, last name, or account. Required
  1. Once you assign a contact, a card will appear with data about that person and the account they are associated with, including a notes section. These notes live with the Account/Contact and are not visible to the customer, so they're a great place to capture information that may be relevant when you're working on a quote. This can prevent CRM data from becoming siloed with a specific account manager or salesperson.
  2. If your quote was created from an email, this field will be suggested based on the originating email address of the forwarded or uploaded email.
  3. For more information on Contacts, including important information like tax rate and payment terms, check out our Accounts and Contacts documentation.
8. Salesperson: The team member handling communication with the buyer. Required
  1. If there is a salesperson associated with the contact/account assigned to the quote, they will automatically be assigned as soon as you select a contact. Otherwise, the salesperson field will automatically populate with whoever first edits the quote.
9. Estimator: The person who will be estimating the cost of the part. Once you assign a team member as the estimator, they'll receive an email alerting them that they are assigned to a quote. Optional
10. Internal notes: Clicking the sticky note icon will open up a text box where you can enter quote-level information. Learn more about quote notes here. Optional

# FAQ

## Why isn't the contact I want to assign showing up when I search from the build-a-quote? They're in my organization's accounts and contacts.

Contacts that are associated with a "Vendor" type account will not be available to assign to quotes. To change an account to the "Customer" type, select the account from your Contacts page and click **Edit**Account info.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/641b51169a0fe82b2d574d65/file-K6ApzIFnsA.png)

Change the type to customer and click **Save changes**to confirm.
