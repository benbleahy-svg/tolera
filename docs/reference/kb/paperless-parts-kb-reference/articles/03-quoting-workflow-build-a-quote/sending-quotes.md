# Sending quotes

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/sending-quotes  
> Topic: Quoting Workflow (Build a Quote)

Once you've finished pricing all of the parts in a quote, send your buyer a digital quote directly from Paperless Parts.

#### On this page

- Previewing the quote
- Finalizing the quote
- FAQ

---

# Previewing the quote

To preview the digital quote that your buyer will receive, select **Preview**from the top right corner of the Build-a-Quote page.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/644295aa741d8f78bef41769/file-v1IwMfb2ec.png)

This will open the digital quote in a new tab, where you can review the prices, part information, and lead times you'll be presenting to your buyer.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6442968c35387a4818397259/file-4KI9niHoyq.png)You can adjust which information is [displayed on the digital quote](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/digital-quote-settings) from your [settings](https://app.paperlessparts.com/settings/digital_quote/terms_and_conditions).

*Note:*Be sure to review the quote expiration date in Quote Details before sending. Once this date passes, your buyer will still be able to view the digital quote, but pricing will be greyed out and they will need to request a requote before placing an order.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6442aa2a59e2f42e804e4b44/file-ibq8St2D7g.png)

# Finalizing the quote

Once you're ready to send the quote, select **Finalize quote**to review the email that will be sent to your buyer.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6442ab5f8d05ee6ec16cf76d/file-eNeDMeW07w.png)

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64429b308d05ee6ec16cf754/file-KWu0JR5ilP.png)Start by reviewing the list of recipients at the top. The email will appear to come from your email address (as the active user sending the quote) and will be sent to[the contact assigned to the quote](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/creating-a-new-quote#assign). The salesperson, the user finalizing the quote, and anybody [marked as a Quote-BCC recipient](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/company-settings#emailnotification) in your [email notification settings](https://app.paperlessparts.com/settings/company/email_notification) will automatically be CC'ed or BCC'ed.

Use the trash can icon to remove a recipient, or search the dropdown menu to add a member of your team to the list.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64429cba59e2f42e804e4b2c/file-NMYBIcmewo.gif)Next, select [an email template](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/creating-email-templates) and make any necessary edits to the subject line and body of the email.

*Note:*The email **must** include a link to the digital quote in order to send it.

If you'd like to attach a PDF of the quote to the email, select **Include PDF in email**at the bottom of the window.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6442a8458d05ee6ec16cf768/file-HgQmwt4nBA.png)

*Note:*Whenever possible, we recommend not including a PDF. Although we can provide analytics on how your customers interact with outstanding digital quote links, we cannot do the same for PDFs. Including the attachment may make it harder for your team to know when to follow up on outstanding quotes.

Once you're satisfied with the digital quote presentation and outgoing email, click **Send quote**.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6442ac3559e2f42e804e4b47/file-nU72dbMe27.gif)

You can track and follow up on outstanding quotes from the [Outstanding tab](https://app.paperlessparts.com/quotes/outstanding) of your Quotes dashboard.

# FAQ

## Why is there a warning at the top of the Finalize quote window?

There are a few reasons you might see a warning at the top of the Finalize quote window. This warning most often appears because a part is missing lead times or was [not marked as complete](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/workflows).

To send the quote, either exit the Finalize quote window and resolve the issue or click **Confirm**to acknowledge the warning.

## Why is Preview/Finalize quote unavailable?

Only users with ["Finalize" permissions](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/team-page-and-user-permissions#permissions) can preview and send digital quotes. You can review a user's permissions from the [Team page](https://app.paperlessparts.com/team).
