# Quoting from PDFs- Tips and Tricks

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/quoting-from-pdfs--tips-and-tricks  
> Topic: Quoting Workflow (Build a Quote)

Although it's no secret that Paperless Parts can provide more automation with CAD files - and that we'd all rather quote from a model - quoting from prints is both a fact of life and a strength that separates job shops from entities like Protolabs.

Here are a few ways you can use our PDF viewer to add consistency and accuracy to your process when quoting off of a drawing.

---

## Gather information and take notes in the PDF viewer

Our PDF viewer was designed to supplement - or even replace - the manual process of printing and marking up prints from an RFQ. Before you start costing, head to the viewer to gather part info that you'll use when estimating a price.

To open a part in the viewer, click the part thumbnail in the Build-a-Quote page or the Part Library.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/641220422291250577b83b86/file-V8FDhBhUi2.gif)

From here, [take measurements](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/pdf-viewer-guide#h.xb3jydb08wo6), [highlight important pieces of information](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/pdf-viewer-guide#h.hwwvhbxcgclr), and [take notes for each annotation](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/internal-collaboration#annotations) in internal collaboration. Notes that you take in internal collaboration will stay with this part going forward, creating a central system of record that you can rely on if you ever need to requote it.

Plus, you can easily reference any notes you take in collaboration from the Build-a-Quote page by clicking the chat icon:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64147fc58d334c46bef37da3/file-kQjtT0X9e8.png)![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64147fdfd43e057e006c73b0/file-noaj5hglVv.gif)

## Enter part information quickly across line items and components

When setting up parts, you'll likely need to enter part number, revision, description, and material, which you may identify from your print. Paperless Parts has a specific tool to make this lightning fast: the **part setup tool,**which is the first tab in the ecosystem app bar on the right side of the screen **when you open the viewer from a quote.**

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001Ec1&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000002voa1)

Values you enter within this tool will sync live to to the open quote, so you can work from both screens at will. When complete entering information for a single part, **you can click "next line item" or "next part" to step to the next line item or component within an assembly.**This will attempt to automatically show you the print of the next part, allowing you to quickly enter key information.

## Apply suggestions for part number and revision

Whenever you upload a PDF print to Paperless Parts, it will attempt to scan the file for likely part numbers and revision, then recommend them to you as suggestions within those fields, from both the Part Setup Tool (found in the viewer opened from a quote), as well as in the line item view.

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000001Ec1&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs0000037YG9)

## Browse beta extractions for description, drawing number, material, and specifications

Whenever you upload a PDF print to Paperless Parts, it will also attempt to scan it for export-controlled key words, ASTM, AMS, and MIL specification keywords, descriptions, drawing numbers, and likely corresponding Paperless Parts materials and present them inside the public-beta "Found in Files" display.

Learn more about this public beta feature [in this article.](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Found-in-files-extractions)

## Keep the viewer open

Because Paperless Parts is browser-based, you can keep multiple pages open at the same time in different tabs or windows. Each tab will have a short descriptor letting you know what you have open.
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64147e5be610cb1dbfd7ad07/file-a5WLrOGkoa.png)

With this in mind, try keeping the Build-a-Quote page and the part viewer open side-by-side while quoting to keep important context readily available.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64148449e610cb1dbfd7ad0f/file-E97NUszUEN.png)

## Enter geometric attributes before you cost

Most shops have some sort of costing logic built into their operations that relies on the geometry of the part. One of the challenges of quoting from PDFs is that we can't automatically extract that information from prints the way we can with CAD files. However, you can still take advantage of costing automation by [entering a part's geometric attributes](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/setting-up-quote-items#h.tdsvpriuna05) before you start costing.

## Perform redactions in Paperless Parts

If you need to get a quote for an outside service as part of the estimating process, you may need to redact sensitive part data before sending prints to a vendor. Rather than relying on Adobe or a cumbersome post-it note process, you can quickly and securely [redact prints in the PDF viewer](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/pdf-viewer-guide#redacting).

For additional security, we recommend sending redacted prints to stakeholders outside of your shop through external collaboration.

## Use the Import historical work feature to reduce manual entry on requotes

Because PDF-only parts require a little more setup at the start of quoting, use the [Import historical work feature](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/import-historical-work-february-22nd-2023) to import a previously quoted part to a new quote. This feature allows you to import an existing part from your library to your quote along with its costing and [quote-item level information](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/setting-up-quote-items#h.upf36qa90bum). Using it will save you from having to reenter geometric attributes or important costing variables.
