# Adding a supplier material

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/adding-a-supplier-material  
> Topic: Setup, Config & Org Settings

Paperless Parts enables you to add your own supplier materials to your account rather than routing through support. If you feel as though a material should be added to the Paperless Parts global materials database, please reach out to support@paperlessparts.com. Paperless Parts would like to add things like color variants, popular heat treats, and entirely new material compositions to its global database.

**1.** Navigate to the materials page within the Configure tab.

**2.** Select the global material that you would like to add a custom option.

**3.** Enter the desired nomenclature and click save.

**4.** Refresh your page and the material will be available for use in quotes.
