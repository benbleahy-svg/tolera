# Email notifications

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/email-notifications  
> Topic: Setup, Config & Org Settings

This setting will designate who in your shop will receive the automated notification and response to the RFQ, quotes and requotes, orders, and payment. Click **Add Email** to include external non-team members to get the same email notifications.

- Quote-Send BCC: Automatically BCC this user on all quotes sent in the platform.
  - Note: The quote-send email will be sent from the verified user who is logged in and sends/finalizes the quote. All other automated emails will be sent from the admin on the account. If the user has not verified their email address, quotes will be from noreply@digital-quote.com.
  - *Note:*The quote-send will automatically CC the user who is logged in and sends/finalizes the quote, and auto BCC the salesperson assigned to the quote. You can review and edit this before sending it out*.*
- Invoice BCC: Includes this user on external order shipment invoice emails sent to your customer.
  - Note: The invoice/shipment emails will automatically CC the user who is logged in and creates the shipment, and auto BCC the salesperson assigned to the quote. You can review and edit this before sending it out.
- Smart RFQ Received: This user will get an internal email every time an RFQ is received from the SmartRFQ form on your website.
  - Note: When a buyer submits through the Smart RFQ form, we will also send an external "RFQ Notification" email to your customer. This setting does not apply to that email, which is instead configured in the "Smart RFQ Settings" section of your settings.
- Email Fwd RFQ Received: This user will get an internal email every time a quote is created via email forwarding.
- Order Confirmation: This user will get CC'd on the internal order confirmation emails which can be sent either:
  - After you facilitate the order in Paperless Parts (and select the checkbox to send the confirmation email to your customer).
  - Or after a customer checks out via the Digital Quote.
  - Note: The salesperson assigned to the quote will automatically be sent the internal Order Confirmation email.
  - Note: There is an external "Order Confirmed" email sent to your customer. This setting does not apply to that email.
- Requote Request: This user will get an internal email if a customer requests a requote of an expired quote.
