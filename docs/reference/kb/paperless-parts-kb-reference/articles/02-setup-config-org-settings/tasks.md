# Tasks

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/tasks  
> Topic: Setup, Config & Org Settings

Assign jobs to members of your team and keep track of what needs to get done with Tasks.

# Assign tasks

Tasks are assigned from the [internal collaboration](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/internal-collaboration) tool. To access internal collaboration, navigate to the Part Viewer and click the chat icon in the top right corner.

Click **Assign Task** at the bottom of the chat. Choose the team member who will own the task and a due date and click **Assign.**Type a message describing the task and press ENTER or click the send icon to confirm.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/61ca0b822c86b66933f56516/file-Mj30QBlW8X.png)

*Tip*: Use the **Add annotation** option to annotate a specific feature or DFM warning on the part and add context to the task.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/61ca0b996e49f25358bc9096/file-G4OHx1x6OZ.png)

The team member you assign will receive a notification via email and in Paperless Parts.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/61ca0c82d1a2f12bbe21eee0/file-gtXgw4d3S2.png)

# Manage and track tasks

Manage and view all current and open tasks right from your [home dashboard](https://app.paperlessparts.com/home) **N****otifications** or from the [Tasks page](https://app.paperlessparts.com/tasks?page=1&status_in=open%2Coverdue). ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64303c5c7bf4bb61c011711c/file-6l4N3ujYWz.png)

*Note:*To access the Tasks page, select **See all tasks**on your home dashboard. ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64303d7691efbc3c6264a73c/file-bW7cXD3s5S.png)

From here, you can filter tasks by their assignee, status, and associated quote number.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64303c76101861213270f997/file-rQb8Z5Ckcg.png)Clicking on a task will take you directly to the part viewer, where you can quickly respond and collaborate with your team.

- Reply and tag your teammate so they get an email notification.
- Edit the task person and due dates.
- Resolve the task when it's been completed.
- Reopen a task and the task will reappear on the home dashboard and notifications.
  - Note: There will be no new email notification for a re-opened task. Please tag your teammate!
- Delete to remove the task from that part file.
  - Note: Deleted tasks are unrecoverable.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/61ca0c15cbdcf5588430ef34/file-8sKQw6vnYk.png)
