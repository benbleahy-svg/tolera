# How to adjust external collaboration settings

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/how-to-adjust-external-collaboration-settings  
> Topic: Setup, Config & Org Settings

**1.** To add someone to a conversation, or to adjust the permissions, go to the top right of the conversation and hit '**Channel Access**' (the folder icon).

**2.** From **Manage Settings**, you can see who has access to this conversation and when they last viewed it. You can also toggle permissions, chat expirations, or end the chat.

**3.**If you'd like to, you can also invite individuals from this modal. Switch to the **Invite People**tab to add their email addresses and a note.
