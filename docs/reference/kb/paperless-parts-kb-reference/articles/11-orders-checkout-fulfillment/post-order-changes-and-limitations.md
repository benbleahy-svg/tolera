# Editing Orders and Post-Order Changes

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/post-order-changes-and-limitations  
> Topic: Orders, Checkout & Fulfillment

If your buyer reaches out after an order has been placed to change information - like the desired shipping address or line item quantity - you can use the Edit Order action to update the associated order.

*Please note that completed or partially shipped orders cannot be canceled on our end. If you have any questions about post-order changes, please reach out to support before completing the order.*

# Editing orders

To make a change to an existing order, navigate to the order and select **Edit Order** from the "Actions" menu.

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000008YMH&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs00000Mhm6L)

*Note:*This action is not available on credit card-based orders or orders with shipments.

This will open a drawer pre-filled with the order info. Make any necessary changes within the drawer and select **Complete Order**to apply the edits.

To notify your buyer that you updated their order, select the **Notify customer of update to order**checkbox on the "Review order" step. This will send the buyer an email summarizing the information on the updated order.

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000008YMH&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs00000MhnyT)

When an order is edited, the order's history will be updated. You can check historical edits by hovering over the icon under an order's number.

![](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000008YMH&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs00000MiYsX)

# Cancel unshipped orders

***U******nshipped*** orders **can be canceled.** This action will initiate a full refund to the customer's credit card for the order. Click Cancel from the orders page, and the order will move to the canceled tab of the orders page.

***Completed*** and ***Partially shipped*** orders **cannot be canceled**. Please work directly with your customer to resolve the refund.

# Updating the quote associated with an order

Once a quote has been finalized, you will be unable to make changes to processes, operations, materials, quote dates, and salesperson / estimator assignments.

If you need to make a post-order quote change, click **Copy**on the orders page**.** This will automatically draft a copy of the associated quote. From here you can make any updates to the quote, send the digital quote to your customer, or facilitate the order.

Alternatively, you can create a quote revision. The link to the original quote can be found in the top left corner of the Orders page. Revising draft quotes puts the original into expired. Revising outstanding quotes will cancel the original, once the new revision is finalized.

[Facilitating an order](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/order-facilitation) allows you the option to select a different quantity, add adjustments (quantity, lead time, and unit price), add additional charges, and update the general checkout process (payment, billing/shipping information). You can facilitate an order on any quote, even the original quote that the order was placed. If the quote is still a draft, facilitating an order will put the quote into outstanding status.

*Note*:Additional charges or added adjustments will automatically update the quote, and DigiQuote. This happens the moment you click **Create Order** (and regardless if the checkout process is completed in full).

# Refunding orders

Canceling an order will initiate a refund/cancellation for the entire order. We do not handle partial refunds or cancelation fees. Please work directly with your customer to resolve any partial refunds.

Canceling orders placed via **credit card**will prompt you to send a refund notification to the customer, at this point you are able to BCC yourself or others. The refund template can be updated from Settings / Email Templates.

Canceling orders placed via **purchase order** and may require a refund. Please note this action will not send a cancelation/refund email. You may need to work directly with your customer to resolve PO cancelations.
