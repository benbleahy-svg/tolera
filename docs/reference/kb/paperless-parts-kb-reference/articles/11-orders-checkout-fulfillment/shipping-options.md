# Shipping options

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/shipping-options  
> Topic: Orders, Checkout & Fulfillment

When an [order is created](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/order-facilitation) in Paperless Parts, there are a few different ways that you or your customer can account for the cost of shipping in the final price.

#### On this page

- Shipping options for digital quote checkout
- Shipping options for facilitated orders
- FAQ

---

# Shipping options for digital quote checkout

There are three shipping options available for your customer to select from when they checkout through the digital quote:

1. Depending on the payment option selected:
  1. Bill Me at Time of Shipment - (Purchase Orders)
    1. Selecting this allows customers to choose from the following shipping methods: Early AM Overnight, Next Day Air, Second Day Air, or Ground.
    2. The shipping charge can be added after clicking Ship + Bill Customer. This shipping charge is only visible on the invoice PDF that gets generated.
  2. Charge Me for Shipping - (Credit Card Orders)
    1. Selecting this allows customers to choose from the following shipping methods: Early AM Overnight, Next Day Air, Second Day Air, or Ground with costs associated for each option. Customer will be charged for shipping at the time the order is placed. Note, a valid shipping address is required for this option to be visible.
2. Use My Shipping Account - Customers have the option to use a shipping account (UPS or FedEX only), select a shipping method, and how to Ship Line Items.
  1. Tracking Number can be inputted when shipping the parts. This will be included in the attached invoice PDF.
3. No Shipping Fees (Includes customer pickup or local delivery if agreed upon) - This is the only shipping option that they can be adjusted described below.
  1. Pickup Recipient field can be inputted when shipping the parts. This will be included in the attached invoice PDF.

You can make changes to your [checkout settings](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/digital-quote-settings#checkout) (including disabling local shipping) from the [Digital Quote section of your Settings](https://app.paperlessparts.com/settings/digital_quote/terms_and_conditions).

# Shipping options for facilitated orders

There are four shipping options available for your team to select from when they facilitate an order in your account:

1. None
2. Depending on the payment option selected:
  1. Bill Me at Time of Shipment - (Purchase Orders)
    1. Select the following shipping methods: Early AM Overnight, Next Day Air, Second Day Air, or Ground.
  2. Charge Me for Shipping - (Credit Card Orders)
    1. Select the following shipping methods: Early AM Overnight, Next Day Air, Second Day Air, or Ground.
3. Use Customer Shipping Account - Customers have the option to use a shipping account (UPS or FedEX carriers only), select a shipping method, and how to Ship Line Items.
  1. Tracking Number can be inputted when shipping the parts. This will be included in the attached invoice PDF.
4. No Shipping Fees - Includes customer pickup or local delivery if agreed upon
  1. Pickup Recipient field can be inputted when shipping the parts. This will be included in the attached invoice PDF.

# FAQ

## How do my/my buyer's selections when creating an order impact an order's "Ship by" date?

The "Ship by" date associated with an order is determined by a quote item's lead time and the time at which an order is created. If the order is placed by 4 PM EST, the current day will count as a production day. If the order is placed after 4 PM EST, the next day will count as the first production day and the "Ship by" date will be a day later.
