# Filtering orders

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/filtering-orders  
> Topic: Orders, Checkout & Fulfillment

Learn how to create and save order filters, as well as export a CSV of filtered orders.

For information on what is included in these exports, or for more information on exporting individual quotes, contacts/accounts, or analytics dashboards, check out our [Export Quotes, Orders, Contacts](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/exporting-quotes-orders-and-accounts-contacts) article.

# Filtering orders

Like the quotes page, you can filter orders on the [Orders page](https://app.paperlessparts.com/orders/all) by company, customer, salesperson, estimator, price, payment status, order dates, and process. Select **S****how Filters**and assign your filter criteria before clicking **Apply Filters.**

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/61b8d59ea5e184696a17aabe/file-j2yMygIt0r.png)After filling out your filter, click **Show Filters,**then **Export**to save results to a CSV file. This export is according to what is filled out on the show filter pop-up.
