# Entering part number, revision, and description

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Entering-part-number-revision-and-description  
> Topic: Part Analysis & Interrogation

## Defining your parts

Assigning a part number, revision, and description helps everyone looking at your parts understand which real-world product they refer to--including your customers. Rigorously defining these fields for parts will help your team find parts, including through search, as well as help your customers check prices on their quotes and purchase orders.

Once you've assigned a part number, revision, and description, you'll be able to find parts based on these fields from everywhere you can search for parts.

---

### What each field is for

1. **Part number**: The customer's part number for your part - typically found on the part's print.
2. **Revision**: The customer's revision for your part - typically found on the part's print.
3. **Description**: The customer's description for your part - typically found on the part's print.

Some or all of these values will be displayed everywhere the part can be viewed inside Paperless Parts, as well as on the digital quote and PDF quotes for your customers.

These values can be changed **only until the part has been sent for the first time - **check that these values are correct for every future use of that part.****

### Field suggestions

The part number, revision, and description fields receive AI-powered suggestions from [Wingman](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/wingman-assistance), Paperless Parts' secure, CMMC-compliant AI--driven by contents of prints or metadata in CAD files.

Clicking on either the fields or the [extractions](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Found-in-files-extractions) on prints will pop the suggestion, which can be navigated with arrow keys and accepted with a click or "enter" key press.
