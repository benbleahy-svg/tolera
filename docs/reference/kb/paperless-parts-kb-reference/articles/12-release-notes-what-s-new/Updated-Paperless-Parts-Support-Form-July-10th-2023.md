# Updated Paperless Parts Support Form - July 10th, 2023

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/Updated-Paperless-Parts-Support-Form-July-10th-2023  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

We've updated the support ticket form in the platform to help us more quickly respond to your requests. As always, you may continue to reach out to the team outside of Paperless Parts via email ( [support@paperlessparts.com](mailto:support@paperlessparts.com)).

Reach out to our support team from anywhere in Paperless Parts by clicking on the (?) icon in the top right corner of the platform:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64a710c8b71911316e400788/file-DpX7K3TtkN.png)

and then clicking "Contact Support":

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64a7132db71911316e40078e/file-x33Lx04HbX.png)

You may then enter the Case Type, Priority, Subject, and Description of your ticket:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/64a711952ab1790663c6bf39/file-mV6KQi7seC.png)

Once you contact our Support team, you will see a message confirming that we have received your request, and that we will respond via email. **The email we will respond to is the same email under which you are logged into Paperless Parts.** Previously you were able to enter a contact email. This option has been removed for security reasons; however, you may indicate in your message if you would like to be contacted in a different manner.

We have also removed the 'Previous Conversations' section of the help window. If you have questions on previous requests you have submitted to our Support team please feel free to reach out, we would be happy to review prior requests.Please let us know if you are having trouble contacting the team, we want to make sure you can reach us!
