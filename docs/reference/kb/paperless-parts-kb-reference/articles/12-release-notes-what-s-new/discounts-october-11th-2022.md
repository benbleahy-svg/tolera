# Discounts - October 11th, 2022

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/discounts-october-11th-2022  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

# What's new?

This release allows you to add discounts to quotes you send through Paperless Parts. Whether you're offering discounts to new customers to get new business, discounting specific processes to fill machines or promote new shop capabilities, or giving salespeople the ability to offer a one-off discount to win a strategic job, discounts can play a key role in helping you win more business. Now, you can implement this quoting strategy and communicate accurate prices to your customers through Paperless Parts.
