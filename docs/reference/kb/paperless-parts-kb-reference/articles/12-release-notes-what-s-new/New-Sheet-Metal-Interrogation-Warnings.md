# New Sheet Metal Interrogation Warnings - July 27, 2023

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/New-Sheet-Metal-Interrogation-Warnings  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

Today we are announcing three new DFM warnings for sheet metal bending.

- "Short Flange" Detection
- "Close Bends" Detection
- "W-bending May Be Required" Detection

# "Short Flange" Detection

 When a given flange’s length divided by the material thickness is lower than a threshold, users will receive a “short flange” warning in viewer interrogations and P3L.

# "Close Bends" Detection

 When the distance between two bends divided by the material thickness is lower than a threshold, users will receive a “close bends” warning in viewer interrogations and P3L. Close bends warnings will trigger for bends within 15° of perpendicular (90°) of the flat surface of the part.This works for bends in both the same and opposite direction bends because the constraint applies to either on a press brake.

# "W-bending May Be Required" Detection

 In a U-shape with 90° bends, when the length of the shortest flange divided by the separation is greater than a threshold, users will receive a “W-bending may be required” warning in viewer interrogations and P3L.
