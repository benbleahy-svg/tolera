# Multi-component sheet metal nesting - November 15th, 2022

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/multi-component-sheet-metal-nesting-november-15th-2022  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

# What's new?

Our new **multi-component sheet metal nesting**module is a powerful feature set that enables you to optimize the cost of material and labor of sheet metal parts by predicting shared cutting patterns of many components out of the same sheet of material.

**Note:** The multi-component sheet metal nesting module (including nest-compatible operations) requires account-specific configuration and setup. To learn more, or to inquire about adding nesting to your account, [reach out to our Support team](mailto:mailto:support@paperlessparts.com).

With multi-component sheet metal nesting, you can now:

- Browse all sheet-metal-nesting-compatible parts within a single quote
- Select components you'd like to nest together and fill out a form to control the nest
- Browse key results from generated nests, including sheet usage, cost distribution, and required stock quantity
