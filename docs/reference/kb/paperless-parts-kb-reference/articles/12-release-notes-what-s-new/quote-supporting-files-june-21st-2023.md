# Quote Files - June 21st, 2023

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/quote-supporting-files-june-21st-2023  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

You can now store files at the quote level within Paperless Parts in addition to the part level. This is useful for storing information that applies to the entire quote package, such as customer emails, multi-part BOM spreadsheets, bid sheets, and more.

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/vT8W5pIUb6t2JfmF8hlrQgiCnAuycV_gN8cGWyA6QoqiIUZGWdeOaZFqFmpV2-eIv5fgXyxAl5_S3rct6QFvIXAgJMXFEAGnMa36tzEdsJhfKMJB9ec61b7_oGJ9z8zOhR8zpUDN95wUz2QTXx7I3M4)

Files attached at the quote level are opened in a stand-alone file viewer, indicating you are viewing a file at the quote level vs the part level.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/opG7pINiFBoTteLsgSK9STp5s-QwW3Y4BNO9yBkRZroKa-5Wk7LxYwMfYIOH2-D6RwXBPQzbVKjVJh0Uc7ERbHGxbMZ1Q5OQQpa_UuKjwk5UzZaetc3lpW3r1vjOtjXYq2cm2ZNyx55a46WzqkMd93g)

**Note:**files uploaded to quote supporting files cannot be directly turned into parts-- **so** **be sure to upload your part files as you normally do:**

- By clicking the "+" button in the quote sidebar
- By clicking "click to upload" at the bottom of the quote
- By dragging and dropping files into the quote sidebar
