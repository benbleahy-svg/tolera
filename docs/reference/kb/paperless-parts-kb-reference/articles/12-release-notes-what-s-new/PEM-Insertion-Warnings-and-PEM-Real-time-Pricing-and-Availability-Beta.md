# PEM Insertion Warnings, and PEM Real-time Pricing and Availability Beta - July 26, 2023

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/PEM-Insertion-Warnings-and-PEM-Real-time-Pricing-and-Availability-Beta  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

Today we introduce two new features that will help you reduce risk and improve pricing and lead time accuracy when quoting sheet metal work that utilizes PEM fasteners.

- PEM Insertion Warnings
- PEM Real-time Pricing and Availability (Public Beta)

# PEM Insertion Warnings

Although PEM fasteners typically make up a small portion of a part's BOM, fastener-related missteps - like drilling the wrong-sized hole, inserting the wrong fastener, or having a hole placed too close to an edge - can have a massive impact on a job's profit. At best, they cause chaos within your shop and require hundreds of hours of work to fix. At worst, they can cause your shop to scrap 100% of the parts you make.

With this feature, we'll alert you to [PEM fastener-specific DFM warnings](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/PEM-insertion-warnings) for sheet metal parts in the purchased component section of the Build-a-Quote page. These warnings will appear for any part that has been assigned a sheet metal interrogation and has child PEM hardware.

# PEM Real-Time Pricing and Availability (Public Beta)

Historically, most shops in Paperless Parts have quoted their hardware two ways: by relying on a stored, historical piece price, or by sourcing direct prices from distributors. With real-time pricing, your shop can access [real-time pricing and fastener availability](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/PEM-real-time-pricing) directly from DB Roberts without having to leave Paperless Parts - or even your quote.

Now, in the component section of assemblies on the Build-a-Quote page, users can see a badge next to PEM fasteners indicating the pricing and availability of that fastener from DB Roberts is being displayed.

![change distributor.gif](/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ns0000004hPl&feoid=00N5G00000WB8Hk&refid=0EMNs000000bXWT)

Users can then review pricing and quantity breaks, as well as inventory availability from different locations within Paperless Parts, and use that to inform pricing and lead time of parts you are quoting.

This feature is in Beta, meaning that it may change over time as we continue development.
