# Single sign-on - March 13th, 2023

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/single-sign-on-march-13th-2023  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

If your shop uses an identity provider (IDP), such as Microsoft Azure Active Directory, you can now implement single sign-on (SSO) for your organization, requiring members of your team to log in to Paperless Parts through your IDP. This feature is part of the Aerospace and Defense package that helps you take control of your shop's security and meet CMMC compliance standards.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/5be4842c04286304a71c0d57/images/6422f2ccb282ac0ba214c02b/file-Kg058qaRyM.png)

Learn more about how to turn on and use single sign-on [here](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/logging-in-to-paperless-parts#h.svyyui259gay).

*Note:*This feature is available as part of the Aerospace and Defense package for Professional and Enterprise pricing tiers. To learn more, reach out to your Customer Success Manager or [our Support team](#) today.
