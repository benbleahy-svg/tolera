# Multi-organizational functionality - December 5th, 2022

> Source: https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/multi-organizational-functionality-december-5th-2022  
> Topic: Release Notes (What's New)

# What's new?

If your company has more than one organization with Paperless Parts, you can now move seamlessly between organizations with the **Switch Organization** action. This eliminates the need to log in and out of different organizations to stay up to date on your team's work.

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/jZ6FRvFQ4VEGpx9BPRuWb7p02IsMIaH2UgWOa8Ju-0J6RqY22Vjoc9-7qZATACW_C8ZeI08NWiAI0M-9_UVmwWr5spbUcfI_J1gMYape-rErSYNyY6RW6MsrhLdjCZ4tm_SAQFSg3b8oW_UQ_jffl1oe8dLtU0babx3A8npNls0DrqwkDKcuW6oSGWuGjA)

Note that this feature needs to be set up by a member of our team. If you're a multi-org user and are interested in this feature, reach out to your Customer Success Manager or [support@paperlessparts.com](mailto:support@paperlessparts.com) to get started.

For a more in-depth guide to this feature, check out our [Functionality for multi-organizational users](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/functionality-for-multi-organizational-users) article.
