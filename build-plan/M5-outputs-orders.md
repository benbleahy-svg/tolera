# M5 — Outputs & Orders

**Spec:** [#milestones](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#milestones) (M5 row) · **Exit criteria (authoritative):** end-to-end — fixture RFQ email → priced quote → send → buyer accepts (PO) → order **with VAT-correct documents, in German**; the send composer shows the triage brief + pre-send checklist; the coaching chip fires on override of a fixture part with **N ≥ 5** historical matches.

**Goal.** Make the thread's tail *real*: the priced quote becomes a **white-label PDF**, a **real email send** from the threaded identity, a **buyer-portal acceptance** (PO checkout with correct DACH VAT), and an **Order** the shop facilitates and ships against — all in German. This **closes the product spine fully real**. Everything before M5 produced a priced quote with a *sent-flag mock*; M5 replaces that flag with PDF + email + portal + order, then layers the four "before-send / during-estimate" AI features (5–8) on top.

**Golden-thread role.** M5 **replaces the thread's sent-flag mock** (`USER-STORIES-AND-WORKFLOWS` quote lifecycle) with a real send → accept → order chain. The digital-quote (M5.1), checkout/VAT (M5.2–M5.3), PDF (M5.4) and send-composer (M5.5) blocks carry the thread; the M1.13 golden-test stays green (pricing figures unchanged — VAT and documents are *added on top* of the verified net figures, never altering them).

**What M5 must NOT do (v1 boundaries — honour these or the block is wrong):**
- **E-invoice export (ZUGFeRD / XRechnung / Peppol / DATEV) is post-v1** ([#milestones](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#milestones) "Deliberately out of v1"). M5 computes VAT and renders the §14-UStG-correct *PDF*; it does **not** emit structured e-invoice XML. The data model carries the fields ([DACH-DELTA-LAYER](../docs/spec/folded-subspecs/DACH-DELTA-LAYER.md) §3) so the post-pilot export block is additive.
- **No order status lifecycle** — orders are ERP-owned; Bid Factory stores the record + nullable `shipped_at`, no Pending/Processing/Shipped progression ([#orderslist](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#orderslist)).
- **Credit-card checkout is v1.x** — checkout is **PO-only** ([#digitalquote](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#digitalquote), [#milestones](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#milestones)). "Charge Me for Shipping (CC)" is hidden.
- **The quote PDF is fully white-label** — org logo + brand colours, **no Tolera branding visible to the customer**. Its design is **owned and implemented here** (M5.4) with WeasyPrint; there is no separate pre-M5 design session (`DECISIONS.md`).

**Sequence:** `M5.0` (estimating shell — chartered 2026-07-17, runs first) → `M5.1 → M5.3 → M5.2` (portal first, then the tax engine it needs, then the checkout that creates the Order) → `M5.4 ⟂ M5.5` (PDF and send-composer can branch in parallel once the portal exists) → `M5.6 → M5.7` (orders list, then facilitate drawer) → `M5.8 ⟂ M5.9` (settings sub-tree and the de-DE catalog are orthogonal). The AI trio `M5.10 → M5.11` lands last (depends on the live send composer). German strings are introduced incrementally per block and consolidated in M5.9.

### M5.3 — VAT & Tax computation (DE/AT/CH + reverse-charge + VIES)   `[M]`
- **Vertical slice:** a quote/order resolves to correct net / tax / gross lines for the buyer's region and tax profile — domestic VAT, intra-EU B2B reverse-charge, or Kleinunternehmer suppression — with the buyer's USt-IdNr validated against VIES.
- **Scope (in):** a **Tax/Region service computed at quote/order level (NOT in Kalk)** ([DACH-DELTA-LAYER](../docs/spec/folded-subspecs/DACH-DELTA-LAYER.md) §3); rate tables by region (DE 19%/7%, AT 20%/13%/10%, CH 8.1%/2.6%/3.8% — **configurable seed, verify at build**); **reverse-charge §13b UStG** (no VAT, both VAT-IDs, the *"Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers"* note) when selling to a VAT-registered business in another EU member state; **VIES validation** of the buyer's USt-IdNr at quote-finalize / checkout, storing result + timestamp; **Kleinunternehmer (§19)** per-org flag that suppresses VAT lines; net-per-rate / VAT-rate / VAT-amount assembly; the §14-UStG mandatory-field set as structured data (consumed by the M5.4 PDF); CH `CHF 1'234.56` vs DE/AT `1.234,56 €` formatting via the Region/Locale service.
- **Scope (out):** **structured e-invoice export — ZUGFeRD/XRechnung/Peppol/DATEV are post-v1** ([#milestones](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#milestones)); the human-readable PDF that *renders* these fields (→ M5.4); GoBD immutable-archive enforcement (data fields here; archival hardening → M6 GDPR/compliance pass).
- **Depends on:** M1.11 (the net VAT lines already compute at quote level — M5.3 generalises them to region + reverse-charge + VIES at order time)
- **Implements (spec):** [#dach-tax](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#dach-tax), [#dach-costing](../docs/spec/Bid-Factory-Build-Spec.html#dach-costing)
- **Internals (provenance):** ../docs/spec/folded-subspecs/DACH-DELTA-LAYER.md (§3 Tax & e-invoicing — authoritative for rates, reverse-charge, §14 fields, VIES, Kleinunternehmer)
- **KB:** [KB: online-checkout-in-paperless-parts](https://help.paperlessparts.com/s/article/online-checkout-in-paperless-parts)
- **Decisions:** log `OPEN:` in DECISIONS.md for the **AT reduced rates (13%/10%)** and **CH reduced rates (2.6%/3.8%)** if not confirmed at build — `DACH-DELTA-LAYER` flags these "(verify)"; do not hardcode an unverified rate as fact.
- **Acceptance criteria:** a German domestic order computes 19% (or 7% reduced) net/tax/gross exactly; an EU-B2B order with a VIES-valid USt-IdNr shows **net only + the §13b reverse-charge note + both VAT-IDs** and charges no VAT; an invalid/absent USt-IdNr falls back to net+VAT+gross; a Kleinunternehmer org shows no VAT line; CH renders `CHF 1'234.56`; the verified M1 *net* figures are unchanged.
- **Test plan (fixtures):** VAT matrix test across DE / AT / CH + reverse-charge + Kleinunternehmer (reuse the CH/CHF fixture from M1.11); a VIES validation test (mocked VIES) storing result + timestamp. Assert gross = net + tax to the cent.
- **Golden-thread role:** makes the thread's order **VAT-correct** — the documents M5.4 renders are now legally framed (§14 UStG) without touching the golden net pricing.
